How To Buy

Get Platform Access

$MEMEBULL is the native utility token of the platform. Hold it to stake for full access to the intelligence suite, earn a share of platform rewards, and vote on protocol upgrades. Buy on Pump.fun, Jupiter, or Raydium — then stake to unlock the engine.

Solana

Pump.fun

Best for buying right at launch, straight from the bonding curve.

  • 1

    Install Phantom or Solflare and fund it with SOL from an exchange.

  • 2

    Go to pump.fun and paste the $MEMEBULL contract address into search.

  • 3

    Click Connect Wallet and approve the connection.

  • 4

    Enter the amount of SOL to spend, confirm, and sign the transaction.

  • 5

    $MEMEBULL lands in your wallet — stake it to unlock the intelligence suite.

Solana

Jupiter / Raydium

Once liquidity moves off the curve, this is the main DEX route.

  • 1

    Open jup.ag (or Raydium) and connect your Solana wallet.

  • 2

    Set the "from" token to SOL and paste the CA into the "to" field.

  • 3

    Check the price impact and slippage, then adjust if the pool is thin.

  • 4

    Confirm the swap and approve the transaction in your wallet.

  • 5

    Stake your $MEMEBULL to start receiving alerts and platform rewards.

Solana

Phantom Wallet

Buy directly inside the Phantom app — no separate DEX site needed.

  • 1

    Install the Phantom extension or mobile app and fund it with SOL from an exchange.

  • 2

    Open the Swap tab inside Phantom.

  • 3

    Paste the $MEMEBULL contract address into the token search field.

  • 4

    Enter the amount of SOL to swap and review the rate and price impact.

  • 5

    Confirm the swap right in Phantom — $MEMEBULL lands in the same wallet, ready to stake.

Always verify the contract address from the official Telegram or X account before buying on any platform — fake tickers and copycat pairs are common on new tokens.
